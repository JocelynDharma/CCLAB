What we know so far about Bugsy? This is a friendly creature that only comes out when it is sunset time where the sun is about to set. It has also been found only in Gunung salak in Indonesia. It really love to interact with humans who are hiking down the mountain. One trick this creature can do is jump at an impressive height. Which makes it very interesting for me as a scientist to study this creature. It would also follow where your finger is pointing which can be seen by moving your cursor around. This creature has a similar characteristic with Capybarra, they have no single thought in mind which is why it is always smiling!
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature For human interaction it follows the mouse cursor and you can move it around to wherever your heart desires as long as it is in within the canvas. This friendly creature can also jump by pressing a spacebar just like in a videogame!
